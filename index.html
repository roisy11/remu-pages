FAQ

The app finds my TV but buttons do not work

On the TV, open Settings > System > Advanced system settings > Control by mobile apps > Network access and set it to Enabled. Also make sure the iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, open the Settings tab and tap Restore purchases. Subscriptions are managed in the iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
