FAQ

The app finds my TV but buttons do nothing

Make sure the iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. In the TV settings, allow control from mobile apps (on some TVs this is called network remote or network control). If the app shows a setup guide, follow it. Then restart the TV and try again.

The app does not find my TV

Make sure the iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. Allow Local Network access for Remu in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network. Turn off VPN on the iPhone. You can also add the TV manually by its IP address.

My TV asks to confirm the connection

Accept the prompt on the TV screen. You only need to do this once.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the app, open the Settings tab and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Open iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions, then choose Remu.
